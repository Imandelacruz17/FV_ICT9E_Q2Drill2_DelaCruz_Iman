Obmontessori

Operation Brotherhood Montessori Center (OBMC) a prominent progressive co-educational school in the Philippines that pioneered the Montessori method of education in the country. Founded in 1966 by Ambassador Preciosa S. Soliven, the institution successfully integrates the child-centered philosophy of Dr. Maria Montessori into the local Asian cultural context. The school systematically guides students from early childhood through senior high school across five strategic branches located in Greenhills, Sta. Ana, Fairview, Las Piñas, and Angeles City. By faithfully adhering to the core philosophies of Dr. Maria Montessori, the curriculum focuses on fostering self-discipline, independence, and unique creative expression to prepare students for real-world civic leadership.
